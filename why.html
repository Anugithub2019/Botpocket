✦ Our builder philosophy

Why we built BotPocket

Agents now read our knowledge, but it is still stored for humans. Three beliefs shape what we build.

01 · Knowledge made for agents

Dropbox syncs files between devices. We sync knowledge between agents.

People read papers and books. Agents need knowledge in a shape made for them: a vector database with a knowledge graph on top. ChatGPT, DeepSeek or any other agent draws from the same connected knowledge, and the graph updates as you add or edit files.

02 · Share only what the question needs

Never hand an agent the whole document.

Pasting a whole paper exposes all of it, costs tokens on every page, and gives the model more room to drift. BotPocket returns only the part your question needs. Fewer tokens means lower cost, and less energy behind every answer.

03 · Knowledge that evolves

Knowledge as a graph can grow, reason and be shared.

A folder sits still. A graph keeps the links between ideas, so agents can reason from one idea to the next, new documents link to what's already there, and you and your agents work on the same knowledge.

Your knowledge, shaped for agents.
